How it is measured

  • Identity: a headless harness pins the clock, camera, film grain and the AO denoise noise, renders 8 fixed views, and diffs every pixel against frames from the pre-optimisation build. Target: 0 differing pixels.
  • Work per frame: triangles and draw calls submitted per frame (all passes), from renderer.info, at the same 8 views.
  • GPU proxy: software-rendered (SwiftShader) frame time at 480×270 — no GPU in the build environment, so this stands in for relative GPU cost.

Where the frame goes (baseline)

  • Vegetation drawn three times per frame (shadow map, water reflection, main) over the whole 400 m map: ~50 % of the frame.
  • Water reflection pass (a second full scene render) + the water shader: ~35 %.
  • Shadow pass + soft-shadow sampling in every lit material: ~30 %.
  • Post-processing (GTAO, denoise, bloom, god rays, FXAA, grade): ~6 %.
  • JS on the main thread: negligible; three.js draw submission ~0.07 ms per draw call.

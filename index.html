Subnautica-inspired browser prototype

Abyssal Bloom

Descend into a stylized alien reef built entirely with canvas rendering. Swim the shallows, scan glowing fauna, pulse sonar through the haze, and surface back at Life Pod 5 once your databank is full.

Swim

Use WASD to glide through the water column. The diver has gentle inertia to keep the movement buoyant.

Boost

Hold Shift for a seaglide-style burst. It drains energy, but it is the fastest way to cross the reef.

Ping

Press Space to send out a sonar pulse. It highlights lifeforms and lets the scene bloom with luminous rings.

Scan

Face a creature and hold E when it is in range. Fill the databank with four unique species, then return to the pod.

This repository was not a Unity project, so the clone has been implemented as a single-file, graphics-first HTML5 canvas experience inside the existing codebase.

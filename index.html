Abyssal Dive

A Subnautica-inspired underwater exploration demo focused on atmosphere: volumetric light, seafloor caustics, drifting particles, swaying kelp, coral shelves, schooling fish, and a scanner-driven discovery loop.

  • Click Dive to lock the pointer.
  • Swim with WASD, boost with Shift, surface with Space, descend with C.
  • Hold E while aiming at a highlighted lifeform or artifact to scan it.

Counter-Strike inspired browser prototype

Bombsite Breach

A compact, no-dependency CS-style clone built in one file. You play the lone counter-terrorist on a retake: clear site A, outduel the defenders, and defuse the bomb before the round timer hits zero.

Move and peek

WASD to move. Hold Shift to slow-walk and tighten your spread when slicing corners.

Aim and shoot

Mouse to aim. Left click to fire. Press R to reload and manage magazines between fights.

Swap weapons

Press 1 for USP-S, 2 for M4A1-S, and 3 for the AWP depending on the angle you want.

Win the round

Eliminate defenders if you must, but the bomb stays live. Reach it and hold E to complete the defuse.

Best played on desktop in a full browser tab. Rounds restart automatically, and enemy pressure ramps up as the score climbs.

Graphics-first Subnautica-inspired prototype

Pelagia Depths

A browser-built underwater exploration clone focused on atmosphere first: layered reefs, caustic light, drifting plankton, kelp forests, glowing alien fragments, and predators stalking the dark while you race your oxygen timer.

Swim the biomes

Use WASD to glide through the Sunlit Shelf, Kelp Expanse, Twilight Garden, and Abyss Edge.

Prioritize visuals

The world is built around lighting, color, and motion: caustics, bubble vents, fauna, and glowing flora.

Scan fragments

Aim with the mouse and hold left click near alien fragments to archive them before predators close in.

Stay alive

Press Space for sonar, hold Shift to burst forward, and recover oxygen at the lifepod or bubble vents.

Designed for desktop browsers in a full tab. This repository is a no-build browser prototype, so the clone is delivered in the current stack with visuals taking priority.

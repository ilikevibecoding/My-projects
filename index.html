Abyssal Dawn

This scene reimagines the repository as a Subnautica-style exploration experience with procedural seabed terrain, animated reefs, roaming sea life, god rays, caustics, scan objectives, and oxygen-driven traversal. Graphics are the priority, so the loop is built around cinematic movement and mood.

Swim freely

WASD to glide through the reef. Use Space to ascend and Shift to dive under coral shelves.

Look around

Mouse look uses pointer lock for smooth first-person swimming and wide panoramic reef views.

Scan fauna and flora

Center a glowing target and hold E to fill the scanner bar, then move to the next beacon.

Manage oxygen

Stay near the surface to refill. Go too deep for too long and the pod will emergency-recover you.

Best experienced in a local server with hardware acceleration enabled. Press Esc to release the pointer, then click the button again to resume.
